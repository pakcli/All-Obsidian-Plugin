# Dictionary: Internet

The **[[internet|Internet]]** is a global network of interconnected [[computer|computers]] communicating via standardized networking protocols (such as TCP/IP, HTTP, and HTTPS).

---

## 🌐 Relation to Obsidian & PakCLI Suite

- **Local vs Cloud**: [[obsidian|Obsidian]] is designed primarily as an offline, local-first application on your [[computer|computer]], meaning you do not need the [[internet|internet]] to read, write, or search notes.
- **Online Features**: An active [[internet|internet]] connection is used when downloading new [[community|community]] [[plugin|plugins]], fetching YouTube video evidence (via YT Extension), or synchronizing notes with a remote Docmost wiki workspace.
