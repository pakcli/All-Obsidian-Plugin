# Dictionary: Symbolic Link (Symlink)

A **[[symlink|Symbolic Link]]** (or Symlink) is a special filesystem entry on your [[computer|computer]] that points transparently to another file or directory on your storage drive.

---

## 🔗 Use in Obsidian

Using **Symlink Manager** in PakCLI Suite, you can link external project folders on your [[computer|computer]] directly into your [[obsidian|Obsidian]] vault without duplicating data or wasting disk space.
