# Dictionary: Obsidian

**[[obsidian|Obsidian]]** is a powerful, local-first knowledge base and note-taking application that stores notes as plain-text [[markdown|Markdown]] files on your local [[computer|computer]].

---

## 🏛️ Key Concepts

### 1. Vault
A **Vault** is a dedicated folder on your local [[computer|computer]]'s storage drive where Obsidian stores your [[markdown|Markdown]] notes, media attachments, and configuration settings (stored in the `.obsidian/` folder). You own your data completely with zero vendor lock-in.

### 2. Wikilinks (`[[Note Name]]`)
Obsidian uses double brackets `[[Note Name]]` to connect notes together, building an interconnected knowledge graph across your thoughts and documentation.

### 3. [[plugin|Plugins]] & [[community|Community]]
Obsidian has an extensible architecture that allows third-party developers in the global [[community|community]] to create custom [[plugin|plugins]] (such as PakCLI Suite) using JavaScript, [[nodejs-and-typescript|TypeScript]], and HTML/CSS.

### 4. Local-First Philosophy
Unlike cloud-only tools, Obsidian operates offline without requiring an [[internet|internet]] connection, ensuring maximum privacy and instant performance.
