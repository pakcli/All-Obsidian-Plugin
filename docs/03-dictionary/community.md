# Dictionary: Community

The **[[community|community]]** refers to the global ecosystem of users, researchers, writers, and software developers who build, share, and collaborate around [[obsidian|Obsidian]].

---

## 👥 Community Elements in Obsidian

- **Community Plugins**: The official registry of third-party [[plugin|plugins]] approved and hosted for Obsidian users over the [[internet|internet]].
- **Community Themes**: User-designed themes and aesthetic customizations for Obsidian.
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Forums, Discord channels, and open documentation where users share vault templates, workflows, and [[markdown|Markdown]] organization strategies.
