# Mobile Installation: iOS & iPadOS

Installation guide for **iPhone** and **iPad** devices.

---

## 🛠️ Method 1: Obsidian iOS App Market

1. Open **[[obsidian|Obsidian]]** on iOS/iPadOS.
2. Open **Settings** (⚙️) -> **[[community|Community]] [[plugin|plugins]]**.
3. Turn off **Restricted mode**.
4. Tap **Browse**, search for `PakCLI Suite`, then tap **Install** and **Enable**.

---

## 📦 Method 2: Manual iOS Files App / Working Copy

1. Open the iOS **Files** app or a sync tool (like *Working Copy* or *iCloud Drive*).
2. Locate your vault folder under `On My iPhone/Obsidian/<Vault>/.obsidian/plugins/pakcli-suite/`.
3. Copy `main.js` and `manifest.json` into `pakcli-suite/`.
4. Relaunch **[[obsidian|Obsidian]]** and enable **PakCLI Suite**.
